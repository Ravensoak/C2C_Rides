Ride History

What began as a challenge in 2011, a Coast to Coast ride with friends, has evolved into a cherished annual tradition, raising funds for multiple charities along the way.

2026 – Llyn Peninsula and Anglesey

Overview

A route around the Welsh Llyn Peninsula passing through Snowdonia and Anglesey.

  • Day 1 - Conwy to Criccieth: A scenic route down the Conwy valley towards Betswy-Coed before a steep climb and then a gradual ascent through Capel Curig to the foot hills of Snowdon. A long descent into Beddgelert before making our way through the Nantmoor valley and onto Porthmadog. A quick diversion to the lovely cove of Borth-y-Gest before making our way past Black Rock Sands and to our finish destination in Criccieth.
  • Day 2 - Criccieth to Nefyn: Quiet country lanes lead to our gradual ascent to Myntho for the spectacular 360 views of the Llyn, before descending to Abersoch. Then quiet country lanes arounds 'Hells Mouth' before the steep climb to Rhiw, but then rewarded with a lovely descent to Aberdaron with far reaching rugged coastal views. Quiet country lanes hugging the coastline took us to Morfa Nefyn where a small excursion took us over the golf course to Porthdinllaen where we tried the beverages at the 'Ty Coch Inn' by the beach. A short stretch back up over the golf course took us to our overnight stop in Nefyn.
  • Day 3 - Nefyn to Beaumaris: We started by ascending to Llithfaen for the striking views across the Llyn rewarded by a fast descent into Llanaelhaearn and then onto quiet country lanes, eventually joining the well paved cycle track to Penygroes for our first cafe stop at Pant Du Vineyard allowing some riders to try their produce!. A small detour from the route due to a closed section of path, but we soon re-joined and headed into Caernafon, circling the castle before heading towards Bangor on the coastal path to our second cafe stop in Y Felinheli. A short ride and we were crossing the Menai Suspension Bridge into Anglesey and to our destination in Beaumaris where some riders did the extra loop to Penmon Point.
  • Day 4 - Beaumaris to Conwy: We left Anglesey by retracing our route from the previous day and crossed the Menai Suspension Bridge. Using cycle paths we rode past Garth Pier and onto Aber Falls distillery for a cafe stop and sampling of their Gins, Liquors and Whiskeys. Carrying on the cycle route which was structurally spectacular at times as it crisscrossed the North Wales Express Way and skirted rocky outcrops, we reached Conwy harbour. From here, most riders crossed over the River Conwy and onto Llandudo before taking the spectacular route around the Great Orme. The ride finished by crossing back into Conwy, where we all met up and had the group photo.

2025 – Scottish Borders

Overview

A route from Gretna Green to Berwick-on-Tweed, following parts of the 'Borderers Way'.

  • Day 1 - Gretna to Carlisle: The ride started in the afternoon with a short route from Gretna Green into Carlisle.
  • Day 2 - Carlisle to Hawick: The route followed quiet country lanes and scenic roads crossing the England / Scotland border before reaching Hawick.
  • Day 3 - Hawick to Wooler: Passing through Kelso allowing us to witness the Women's Tour of Britain before skirting the Cheviot Hills and arriving in Wooler.
  • Day 4 - Wooler to Berwick on Tweed: A detour to the spectacular Bamburgh Castle before taking the coastal path into Berwick. Lindesfarne not visited due to tidal timings.

2024 – Scotland

Overview

A circular route starting and ending near Edinburgh, passing through Stirling and Glasgow.

  • Day 1 - Champany to Stirling: The ride started by visiting the 3 bridge viewpoint in Queensferry before crossing the Forth road bridge and riding the shoreline of the Firth Estuary making use of cycle paths through Dunfermline to Stirling.
  • Day 2 - Stirling to Glasgow: The route headed to Balloch on the shores of Loch Lomond, where some riders chose the detour to cross by ferry from Balmaha to Luss, before continuing along cycle paths beside the River Leven to Dumbarton, then turning east into Glasgow to follow the River Clyde.
  • Day 3 - Glasgow to Champany: The return to Champany traced quiet cycle paths and a sweeping stretch beside the Forth and Clyde Canal, with opportunities to pause at the Falkirk Wheel, admire the Kelpies, and explore the historic Blackness Castle along the way.

2023 – Cotswolds

Overview

A series of routes around the Cotswolds.

  • Day 1 - Lenches Circluar: The ride started by visiting the Lenches continuing on to Bidford-on-Avon crossing over the river bridge to visit Dorsington with all of its Thatched roof properties. We continued following the pretty lanes onto Broadway visiting Tisanes tea room for lunch in the sun trap back garden. Next we visited Toddington station before continuing onto Beckford & the lanes back to the start.
  • Day 2 - Chipping Campden Circular: Starting out from Chipping Campden we followed the lanes to Moreton in the marsh conquering the 18% climb over the ridge on the way, before continuing onto Daylesford organic farm shop for refreshments. Next we visited Bourton on the water following the route back to the start via lower & upper slaughter (some of us risking cycling through the ford).
  • Day 3 - Bretforton Circular: Most people travelled back home leaving Martyn, Kev & Heather to ride to Stratford upon Avon before returning to the fleece inn at Bretforton for refreshments.

2022 – Bath to Belper

Overview

A route making its way from Bath to Bristol, then over the Severn Estuary, across the Malvern Hills and through Worcester, Birmingham and Lichfield before arriving back in Belper.

  • Day 1 - Midford to Bristol: The ride started in the afternoon from Midford using an old railway line with many tunnels and ending in Bristol.
  • Day 2 - Bristol to Great Malvern: The route out of Bristol followed the River Avon to the Clifton Suspension Bridge before heading North West using the the Severn Bridge to get to Chepstow. It then used the Wye Valley Greenway navigating the Tidenham Tunnel (bat colony) passing Tintern Abbey and onto Ross-on-Wye, before crossing the Malvern Hills into Great Malvern.
  • Day 3 - Great Malvern to Lichfield: The route took us via cycle paths through the centres of Worcester and Birmingham before reaching Lichfield.
  • Day 4 - Lichfield to Belper: The route took us from Lichfield back home to Belper calling at the World Peace cafe en-route and riding a long stretch on the Great Northern Greenway.

2021 – Conwy to Belper

Overview

A route starting in Conwy following the north coast of Wales, before making its way inland to the Welsh borders, Shropshire and Staffordshire before arriving in Belper.

  • Day 1 - Conwy to Corwen: The ride started started from Conwy travelling North to Llandudno with a scenic ride around The Great Orme, before following the coastal path to Rhyl, and then in land through the rolling hills of Denbighshire on to Corwen.
  • Day 2 - Corwen to Eccleshall: The route took us through scenic country lanes to Llangollen and across the Pontcysyllte Aqueduct to Chirk, before passing Ellesmere Lake and onto Eccleshall.
  • Day 3 - Eccleshall to Home: The route took us into Staffordshire and through the Churnet Valley and back home to Belper.

2019 – Isle of Wight / New Forest

Overview

A route starting in Lymington to allow a ride around the Isle of Wight, before taking roads through the New Forest and the Wiltshire and Gloucestershire countryside ending in Cirencester.

  • Day 1 - Lymington and Isle of Wight: The ride started in Lymington, taking the ferry to the Isle of Wight to ride an anticlockwise circular route around the island before catching the ferry back to Lymington.
  • Day 2 - Lymington to Beckington: The route took us through the New Forrest and quiet country lanes through Wiltshire passing Salisbury and Longleat before arriving in Beckington.
  • Day 3 - Beckington to Cirencester: The route used quiet country lanes in Gloucestershire passing through Bradford-on-Avon with an end point in the Roman town of Cirencester.

2018 – South Wales to North Wales

Overview

A challenging route starting on the South Wales coast in Llanelli, passing the side of the Brecon Beacons and the Elan Valley, through the heart of Wales and Snowdonia to finish in Conwy on the North Wales coast.

  • Day 1 - Llanelli to Llandovery: The ride started in the afternoon from Llanelli on a disused railway line, passing Carreg Cennon Castle before reaching Llandovery.
  • Day 2 - Llandovery to Staylittle: The route took us through Builth Wells and Rhayader before a challenging few climbs to reach our accommodation at the 'Weather Station' hostel in Staylittle.
  • Day 3 - Staylittle to Porthmadog: Heading North West from Staylittle, this route had a steep climb after Machynlleth passing Corris before descending to Dolgellau and along the Mawddach Trail, crossing the Barmouth Railway bridge and through Harlech onto Porthmadog.
  • Day 4 - Porthmadog to Conwy: The route took us through Ffestiniog and up to the Ffynnon Eidda viewpoint before descending towards PenMachno and Betws-y-Coed and then following the Conwy valley down to the coast at Conwy.

2017 – Coast to Coast

Overview

One of the classic Sustrans Coast to Coast routes from the Irish Sea to the North Sea, crossing the Lake District, Pennines, and Durham Dales.

  • Day 1 - Whitehaven to Penrith: The route starts on the West coast and took us through the Northern part of the lake district, over Whinlatter Pass and into Keswick. It should take the scenic Threlkeld railway path but flood damage forces a detour past Castlerigg Stone Circle with the first day ending in Penrith.
  • Day 2 - Penrith to Allenheads: A few climbs including Hartside Pass and we were into wild morland of the West Penines. Passing by Garrigill Falls and onto Nenthead before climbing to our destination Allenheads in the heart of the Pennines.
  • Day 3 - Allenheads to Sunderland: A steep climb out of Allenheads leads across the county border to a beautiful descent to Stanhope before another steep climb to Waskerley. From here it is mostly downhill and cyclepaths into Sunderland finishing on Roker Beach.

2016 – Reivers Way

Overview

This is a Sustrans route from Whitehaven to Tynemouth tracing the historic paths of the Border Reivers - raiders who operated along the Anglo—Scottish border from the 13th to 17th centuries.

  • Day 1 - Whitehaven to Carlisle: The route makes its way inland to the North West part of the Lake District with Carlisle being our Day 1 destination.
  • Day 2 - Carlisle to Bellingham: Soon after Carlisle we make our way to the Scottish Border where we ride through wild rugged countryside passing Kielder Water before reaching our final destiantion in Bellingham.
  • Day 3 - Bellingham to Tynemouth: The route passes through beautiful countryside before arriving at the North Sea coast above Whitley Bay. From here, it is a short route down the coastal road to Tynemouth.

2015 – Hadrians Wall Cycleway

Overview

This is a Sustrans route from Ravenglass to South Shields following the line of Hadrian’s Wall from the Irish Sea to the North Sea, offering a blend of Roman history, scenic countryside, and quiet roads.

  • Day 1 - Ravenglass to Silloth: The route starts at the roman fort in Ravenglass and follows the coastal path behind Sellafield Nuclear Plant and then skirting the North West part of the Lake District passing through Cockermouth and onto our Day 1 end destination of Silloth.
  • Day 2 - Silloth to Once Brewed (Haltwistle): Following the line of the wall, the route takes us through the heart of Carlilse before heading to the North Pennines and our day 2 destination of Once Brewed near Haltwhistle.
  • Day 3 - Once Brewed (Haltwistle) to South Shields: The route starts by passing the roman excavations of Vindolanda before following the line of the wall through spectacular countryside to Hexham, before descending into South Shields - the end point of the route

2014 – Coast and Castles

Overview

The Coast and Castles cycle route is a Sustrans long-distance ride from Newcastle to Edinburgh, following the Northumberland and Scottish coastlines past historic castles, fishing villages, and dramatic scenery.

  • Day 1 - Newcastle to Seahouses: The group left Newcastle leaving Kev behind to meet Martyn at the train station who both starting cycling 90mins behind the main group. Everyone taking the same route out to the coast at Tynemouth continuing with the ride hugging the coast through Whitley Bay, Blyth, Amble. Eventually everyone regrouped for lunch at Alnmouth before continuing to finish the ride at Seahouses.
  • Day 2 - Seahouses to Dunbar: After leaving Seahouse and and passing Bamburgh Castle, 3 riders pressed on to visit Lindisfarne on Holy Island (Laurie, Mark & Martyn) - the main group choosing to continue along the coastal path. All riders regrouped for lunch in Berwick upon Tweed, before continuing on to the Day 2 stopover in Dunbar.
  • Day 3 - Dunbar to Edinburgh: The day started with breakfast in the conservatory with a great view of the seals basking on the rocks in the early morning sun. The riders left Dunbar and followed the coast to Musselburgh for a relaxed lunch before continuing at a steady pace onto our final destination of Edinburgh.
